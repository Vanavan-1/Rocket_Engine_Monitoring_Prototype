---

### Problem Statement We Choose

   Build ultra-efficient, self-sustaining systems for a six-month voyage where every joule, byte and gram matters — keeping the crew powered, supplied, and mentally engaged inside a closed spacecraft

---

## ABOUT THE PROJECT 

We are Going to Monitor the Cryogenic Engine's Health , These are Things We are Going To  Monitor 
 1) The Temperature
 2) Vibration level
 3) Valve Position
 4) RPM of the fuel pump of the engine and And Controlling the RPM By Adjusting a Control Valve Position
